# storyboard-writer

**When:** concept chosen; before plates/compose.
**Inputs:** concept, assets list, length, music tempo, never-claim list.

## Beat table columns
# · time (or bars) · purpose · on screen · proven (source of truth) · words (≤ 7 per line) · sound · duration · transition-in.
7–14 beats for 15–60 s. Put every beat start on a **bar line** of the music (90 BPM → bar = 2.667 s) so the cut,
the music and the cut-downs line up.

## Rules with numbers
- First 3 s: hook + finished frame 1. Brand ≤ 5 s. One CTA at the end.
- Each text line visible ≥ 1.8 s; reading speed ≤ 17 characters/s.
- No shot > 8 s without a new move or framing.
- The product you sell leads every scene: a mood visual of a *different* drink gets ≤ 2 bars (film 2 judge).
- Text never covers the product.
- Cut-downs = whole-bar segments of the hero (see `studio/film2/config.mjs`).

## Proof list / honesty list
Proof: every number or claim → where it is read back from (API, DOM, owner's own photo). Honesty: what is staged
or approximate and the on-screen label for it. If there is nothing to prove (a café ad), write the
never-claim list instead and keep copy to sensory/emotional lines.

## Copy (Egyptian Arabic, elegant)
Short, warm, no slang, no superlatives. Film 2 lines: "مش أي ماتشا…" · "جوّاها إيه؟" · "خُد لحظتك… والدنيا تستنى." ·
"قعدتك الحلوة… مستنياك." · CTA "جرّبها النهارده".

## Checklist
1. Beats on bar lines. 2. ≤ 7 words/line. 3. Hook ≤ 3 s. 4. Brand ≤ 5 s. 5. Dwell ≥ 1.8 s. 6. Longest shot ≤ 8 s.
7. Transition per beat named. 8. Sound per beat named. 9. Proof/never-claim list. 10. Owner decisions listed.
