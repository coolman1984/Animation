# data-honesty

## Rules
1. Every number, UI screen and claim comes from a read-back (API, DOM, file, the owner's own photo) or carries a
   staged label ("منتج تجريبي", "بيانات تجريبية", "الوقت مضغوط", "اسم تجريبي").
2. The cut refuses to build when a shown figure differs from its source (throw in the actor skill / cut step).
3. Each film has a **never-claim list** in its BRIEF (film 2: best/first/only, 100 % natural, prices, hours,
   "since 1930", partnership with the carton maker, address until supplied).
4. Product descriptions only as far as the photo/owner confirms (film 2: "matcha · coconut milk · boba" — flagged for the owner to confirm the recipe).
5. Third-party brands visible in owner photos: shown as-is, no claims, listed as an owner decision; offer to blur.
6. No real people's face/voice without explicit permission; no scraping.
7. The final report lists what is staged, unverified, approximate.

## Checklist
1. Proof list or never-claim list exists. 2. Each on-screen number traced. 3. Labels on staged items.
4. Mismatch throws. 5. Copy has no superlatives. 6. Third-party marks listed. 7. Owner decisions listed.
8. Recipe/ingredients confirmed or flagged. 9. Report has the honesty section. 10. ASSETS.md complete.
