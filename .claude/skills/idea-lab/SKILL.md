# idea-lab

**When:** the owner gives a subject but no concept. Skip when they already dictated the concept (film 2).
**Inputs:** subject, viewer, one action, platforms, never-claim list.

## Formats (5 cards each = 30)
problem→proof demo · day in the life · before/after · myth-buster · numbers story · behind the scenes.
Card = hook (first 2 s) · single promise · proof shot · payoff · CTA · platform · length.

## Hook library
pattern-break (frame 1 already mid-action) · number-first · question ("جوّاها إيه؟") · before/after split ·
"watch this" cold open. **Never open on a logo** — but get the brand on screen by 5 s (the product can carry it:
film 2's cup logo is readable from frame 1).

## Scoring (1–5 each, cost 5 = cheapest)
clarity in 3 s · proof strength · emotional pull · production cost · reusability. Show the table sorted by total,
recommend three: the winner + one that is cut from the same footage + the next film.

## Platform fit
FB/IG feed 4:5 (largest feed area) · Reels/Stories 9:16 (safe: top 14 %, bottom 35 %, sides 6 %) ·
YouTube 16:9 · bumper ≤ 6 s. Meta: 6–15 s versions usually win; keep the long hero but always cut short versions.

## Pitfalls
- A card whose proof needs a claim you can't verify (prices, "best", savings) is blocked — mark it ✗.
- Ideas that need footage you don't have (people, places) cost more than they score.

## Checklist
1. 30 cards. 2. Every card has a proof shot. 3. Claims checked against never-claim list. 4. Scores filled.
5. Sorted table. 6. Three picks with reasons. 7. Winner's hook ≤ 2 s. 8. Brand ≤ 5 s. 9. Platforms named. 10. Saved to `<film>/IDEAS.md`.

## Worked example
`studio/film1/IDEAS.md` — 30 cards for a shop-ordering app; winner A1 "One order, two screens" (22/25).
