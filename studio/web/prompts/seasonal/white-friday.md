**Seasonal offer page (White Friday, Ramadan, back to school). Direction: clear deals, no fake urgency.**
- **Two panels.** In Arabic the reading panel is on the right (inline-start) and the picture panel on the left (inline-end) — write it with
  grid order and logical properties only (`inline-start/end`), never `left/right`, so the English version flips by itself.
- Reading panel: the season tag, promise, then one offer per screen: name, one line, price now, price before (struck), the discount,
  «أضف إلى السلة». **The picture panel is sticky and swaps to the product of the offer under the reading line** (the hero motion).
- Phone: the picture becomes a sticky strip under the header (≈ 34 % of the screen) and the menu moves to a **bottom tab bar** in thumb reach.
- Honesty: "before" prices must be the client's real previous prices; an end date is written; no countdown timers that restart.
- Almarai; white, one blue action colour, sale red only on the discount.
