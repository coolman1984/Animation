**Café / drinks brand with one hero product. Direction: warm, tactile, the product turning in your hand.**
- Header: wordmark, two links, one order button. The first screen splits: a sticky stage with the **3D bottle built in code** (Three.js primitives,
  no image or downloaded model) and, beside it, the stations: promise → bean → steeping → bottle.
- **Hero motion:** the bottle turns one full revolution across the stations; a small detail moves only at the station it explains (beans at "bean").
  It is drawn by scroll position, so it never plays on its own. **Still version:** reduced motion, data saver, ≤ 2 CPU cores or ≤ 2 GB memory,
  or no WebGL → an inline SVG bottle with the same label. Write both states in DESIGN.md §C.
- The one interaction: choosing a flavour recolours the bottle label (and the still SVG). Prices only from the client.
- Rubik, one family; coffee brown, cream, one burnt-orange action colour.
- Phone: the stage becomes a sticky strip (≈ 46 % of the screen) above the stations; the bottle stays visible while reading.
