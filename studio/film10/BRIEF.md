## Direction
- **Hero:** the blue pixel of the logo's i-dot travels through every chapter and builds the logo at the end (1,699 traced pixels).
- **Light theme:** warm paper white, ink #1F2328, brand blue #0A66FF / cyan #18B6FF / navy #0732AF, soft contact shadows, a faint pixel grid.
- **Type** (two families):
  - Display: El Messiri (Arabic), paired with Instrument Serif italic (Latin accents).
  - Text/UI: IBM Plex Sans Arabic.
- **Motion:** springs and blur morphs (lib/uimorph.js), words rise from blur, a cursor narrates actions, slow push-ins on holds,
  and one real-3D chapter (Three.js: TV + phone in a light studio).
- **Sound:** an original 80 BPM score (bar = 3 s) in D major, mixed low and smooth at −18 LUFS: soft piano, harp arpeggios,
  string pads, a gentle pulse in the middle, bells on the logo. Very light UI foley.
