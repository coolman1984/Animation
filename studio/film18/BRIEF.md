## Direction
ميزان = the scale. The shop's loads tip the beam; the copper bar slams in under it and levels it — that is the logo. The same copper bar
then does every job: scanner laser, total, paid button, shelf, instalment progress, tallest sales bar — and returns into the logo.
Worlds: night (problem) → cream tile → ivory (sell) → night shelves → copper (instalments) → night numbers → ivory payoff.
Sound: original 120 BPM electro-pop; F minor tension → A♭ major drop at the logo; one hook returns on a new instrument every chapter;
the scanner beeps play the hook's notes; a low wavering warning bell under the question.
