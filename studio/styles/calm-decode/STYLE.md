# Calm Decode (no music) — style card
> A calm, scientific explainer told by drawing and sound effects only: one case, its causes, the method, the brand's motto.
> Source film: film 9 (NeuroAnara). Route: **bespoke** (rung 3: `film9/`).

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- Every line from the client's own posts; one case (a patient who cannot stand) decoded into its causes and a method.
- Not a lecture slide deck, not stock medical imagery, not a music-bed ad (the owner asked for no music here).

## 2. Materials & rendering
- A figure drawn in code in the posts' own style; dendrites and pulses along an axon as the connective motif; soft glow only on the signal.

## 3. Colour logic
- The page's calm palette (deep blue-green ink, teal signal, warm paper); one gold accent for the decoded word.

## 4. Type
- A serif wordmark (Lora) as the page writes it; Arabic copy in a clean sans; formal lines kept in their original language.

## 5. Motion
- Slow, legible: one action per beat; a strike-through then the corrected motto letters decode one by one.

## 6. Camera grammar
| Move | Expresses | Serves |
|---|---|---|
| Locked on the figure | the problem | hook |
| Pull back as causes grow | scale of the question | causes |
| Follow the pulse | the method | steps |

## 7. Sound palette (no music)
- One effect per on-screen event, a very low room tone, soft-limited peaks. A low wavering warning bell for "I have a problem and I'm worried"
  (the owner chose a symbolic sound over literal breaths or creaks).

## 8. Native moves
- The struck sentence decodes into the motto. The pulse lights each step of the method.

## 9. Pitfalls
- Literal foley (rejected). Reading time too short for medical terms. Loudness collapses without music: every beat needs its effect.

## 10. How to build it here
- Rung 3 from `film9/`; sound with `lib/sfx.mjs` (Openverse first for effects). Engine approximation: `music: false` spec with `path` and `words`.

## 11. Variation space
- Any case-based teaching page: law, finance, coding errors. 15–25 s Reels.
