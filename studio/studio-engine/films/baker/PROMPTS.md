# عم حسن والفرن — painted short (owner idea brief, 2026-10-27)

Format: 9:16 vertical, ~40 s, oil painting look. Images: ChatGPT Image / Google Gemini (owner's accounts). 8-second clips: Google Veo (image → video).
Studio does the rest: depth layers, camera, captions, narration, music, gates.

## Script (Egyptian Arabic narration, one line per shot)
1. كل يوم قبل الفجر… عم حسن بيصحى قبل الشارع كله.
2. يولّع الفرن اللي ولّعه أبوه قبله بأربعين سنة.
3. إيديه عارفة العجينة… من غير ما يبص.
4. الناس فاكرة إن العيش بيتعمل بالدقيق والمية.
5. بس هو عارف إن السر… في الصبر.
6. ساعتين يستنى العجينة تصحى زيّه.
7. ولما الشمس تطلع… الحارة كلها تصحى على ريحته.
8. الحاجات الحلوة… عمرها ما اتعملت بسرعة.

## Style bible (paste at the end of every image prompt)
STYLE: A contemporary narrative oil painting on linen canvas. Thick, confident impasto brushstrokes you can see, palette-knife
texture in the light areas, soft lost edges in the shadows. Warm amber and honey-gold firelight against deep teal and indigo shadows,
a limited, harmonious palette. Cinematic composition with one clear focal point and strong value contrast, quiet and emotional,
the feeling of a museum-quality painting rather than a digital render. Vertical 9:16 frame. Keep the top third of the frame calm and
simple (sky, wall or shadow) for subtitles. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signs with writing, no logos, no watermark, no frame,
no border. Avoid: photorealism, 3D render look, plastic skin, extra fingers, distorted hands, cartoon style, oversaturated colours.

## 0. Character sheet (make this FIRST, then attach it as the reference for every shot)
Character reference sheet, oil painting: an Egyptian baker in his late sixties named Hassan, warm brown skin, short neat white beard,
kind tired eyes with deep smile lines, thick grey eyebrows, a beige knitted skullcap, a faded off-white cotton galabeya with the sleeves
rolled to the elbow, strong forearms dusted with flour, a worn brown leather apron. Three views side by side on a plain warm-grey
painted background: front, three-quarter, profile; plus one close study of his flour-covered hands. Same painting style everywhere.
+ STYLE

## Shots (attach the character sheet; say "keep Hassan exactly as in the reference")
1 — Hook (wide): Before dawn in a narrow old Cairo alley, deep blue night, one warm light glowing from a small bakery doorway at the end of
the alley; Hassan, small in the frame, seen from behind, walks toward the light carrying a key ring; wet cobblestones reflect the glow,
balconies and hanging laundry silhouetted above. + STYLE

2 — The oven (medium): Inside the old bakery, Hassan kneels to light a huge domed brick oven, his face lit from below by the first orange
flames, the brick arch blackened by decades of fire, an old framed photograph of a younger baker barely visible on the wall in shadow.
+ STYLE

3 — Hands (close-up): Extreme close-up of Hassan's weathered hands kneading a large pillow of dough on a wooden table dusted with flour,
flour drifting in the warm light like snow, his eyes not visible, only hands, dough and light. + STYLE

4 — Everyone else (wide): The same alley at blue hour from a high window looking down, other houses still dark and asleep, a cat on a
wall, only the bakery window glowing gold — a lonely, peaceful moment. + STYLE

5 — The turn (portrait): Hassan sits on a low wooden stool beside the rising dough covered with a cloth, hands resting on his knees,
eyes closed, a soft smile, waiting; firelight on one side of his face, cool dawn light from a small window on the other. + STYLE

6 — Time (still life): Rows of round dough loaves resting on long wooden boards, slightly risen, a single beam of early light crossing
them through dust in the air, Hassan's shadow on the wall behind. + STYLE

7 — Sunrise (wide): Sunrise over the alley, golden light pouring between the old buildings, neighbours opening their shutters and
doors, a child running with an empty basket toward the bakery, steam and warm smoke rising from the bakery chimney. + STYLE

8 — Landing (medium): Hassan in the bakery doorway handing a basket of fresh round baladi bread to a small girl, both smiling, steam rising
from the bread, the whole frame bathed in warm morning gold, a calm, complete ending. + STYLE
